Ludo Khel

Five classic Indian board games in one beautiful app.

The games

  • Ludo (Pachisi) — 2 to 7 players, classic and radial boards
  • Snakes & Ladders (Moksha Patam) — the original game of virtues and vices
  • Chaupar — the royal game with pasa stick dice, partnerships and melds
  • Chowka Bara — the 5×5 cowrie-shell race
  • Ashta Chamma — the deeper, sharper 7×7 cousin

Made with care

  • Three hand-tuned board palettes, light and dark
  • Real 3D dice, pasa sticks and cowrie shells
  • Pass-and-play or against the computer
  • Hindi and English
  • 100% offline — no ads, no tracking, no accounts
